# Section 1 to Section 2 workflow

## Repositories

- Section 1: https://github.com/romenmeitei/AISKG_01_Framework
- Section 2: https://github.com/romenmeitei/AISKG_02_Framework

## Deterministic manuscript workflow

1. Run the Section 1 notebook with `RUN_MODE = "MANUSCRIPT_SNAPSHOT"`.
2. Confirm that `PIPELINE_SUCCESS.txt` is present in the Section 1 output.
3. Download `Mushroom_KG_Reproducibility_Inputs_v2_from_upstream.zip`.
4. Open the Section 2 notebook.
5. Either allow Section 2 to auto-download its frozen companion ZIP or upload
   the bridge ZIP generated by Section 1.
6. Run all Section 2 cells.
7. Confirm that Section 2 reports 94/94 expected-result checks and creates
   `PIPELINE_SUCCESS.txt`.
8. Archive the final output ZIP and record the Git commit and release tag used.

The bridge includes Section 1-regenerated corpus/theme, entity, relation, and
edge tables together with the fixed expert-validation and held-out benchmark
inputs required by Section 2.

## Live refresh

A live refresh produces a dated update rather than an exact reproduction of the
historical manuscript snapshot. Complete the generated expert topic-review CSV
before running `APPLY_CURATION_AND_EXTRACT`. Do not overwrite the frozen
manuscript snapshot with live-refresh outputs.
